**Leonida Nights Cover Press** — an offline web app that turns your photo and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art: a multi-panel poster with bold lettering, a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, grain and a vignette, exported as a PNG to share. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6 Cover Art” bounty, $250 in USDC.

Load up to four photos (one per panel, or one photo across all panels), drag and zoom each to crop it, pick one of four layouts — Hero + Three, Four Panel Grid, Full Bleed, Tilted Strip — and one of five grades — Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Ocean Teal, Heat Haze, Vice Noir. Edit the title, subtitle and ribbon badge, then export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline in desktop Chrome; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
